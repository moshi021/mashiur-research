Materials focus

Why high-entropy alloys?

High-entropy alloys are multi-principal-element alloys whose large compositional space can produce unusual combinations of mechanical, thermal, and irradiation-related properties.

Unlike conventional alloys built around a dominant solvent element, HEAs distribute chemistry across several principal elements, creating a much broader design space for materials discovery.

More compositional freedom. More design space. More room for data-driven discovery.

Selected work

Designing sustainable high-entropy alloys with machine learning.

My work combines machine learning, sustainability metrics, and multi-objective Pareto optimization to explore high-entropy alloy compositions. The study screened 50,000 virtual compositions and identified a high-hardness, lower-embodied-energy candidate.
