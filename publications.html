Pareto-optimization of sustainable high-entropy alloys: balancing hardness and sustainability via machine learning

Md. Mashiur Rahman, Md. Mohminul Islam, S. M. Nasim Rokon & Md. Mostafa Kamal
Journal of Materials Chemistry A

Random Forest + sustainability metrics + Pareto optimization across 50,000 virtual compositions. Champion: Fe₀.₅₅Al₀.₂₅Si₀.₁₇Mn₀.₀₂Ni₀.₀₂, ~1126 HV and 87 MJ kg⁻¹ embodied energy.

High-throughput prediction of Debye temperature in high-entropy carbides via machine learning on physics-derived targets

Md. Mashiur Rahman & S. M. Nasim Rokon
Materials Today Communications

XGBoost predicts Debye temperature for 495 HECs using 19 compositional descriptors, achieving R² = 0.96 and RMSE = 15.71 K, with average atomic number and atomic mismatch emerging as dominant predictors.

Toward a glass-box framework: Interpretable and statistically validated machine learning for geopolymer concrete strength prediction

Md. Mashiur Rahman
Materials Today Communications

An interpretable XGBoost framework trained on 1,470 mix designs achieves R² = 0.95, RMSE = 3.40 MPa and MAE = 2.04 MPa. SHAP identifies curing time, GGBFS content, and NaOH molarity as dominant drivers.

Emergent compositional control of martensitic transformation in NiTi-like shape memory alloys revealed by manifold learning

Md. Mashiur Rahman, Pabitro Prosad Mondal & Mirza Humaun Kabir Rubel
Discover Materials

A curated 518-alloy dataset is analyzed using physically motivated descriptors and manifold learning. The fifth diffusion coordinate, ψ₅, shows the strongest correlation with martensitic start temperature and is reconstructed as an interpretable emergent variable.
