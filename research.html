Research directions

Four connected research themes.

My research brings together machine learning, computational materials science, and materials physics to understand and design compositionally complex materials.

01
Machine Learning · Materials Informatics

Machine Learning for materials discovery, design, optimization, and structure–property relationships.

My research focuses on using machine learning to accelerate materials discovery and design, while uncovering interpretable structure–property relationships that provide insights into the underlying materials behavior.

Materials Informatics Alloy Design Explainable AI Optimization
02
HEAs · HECs · RHEAs

High-Entropy Alloys and Ceramics.

High-entropy materials offer enormous compositional spaces and emergent behavior. My work explores data-driven prediction and optimization of their mechanical, thermal, and functional properties, with particular interest in high-entropy alloys, refractory systems, and high-entropy ceramics.

HEAs RHEAs HECs Mechanical Properties Thermal Properties
03
Chemical Short-Range Order · Interpretable ML

Machine learning for understanding chemical short-range ordering in HEAs.

I am interested in understanding how local chemical environments and short-range ordering influence the behavior of multi-principal element alloys. The goal is to develop quantitative descriptors that connect local chemical structure with macroscopic materials properties.

SRO Warren–Cowley Parameters Pair Correlations Local Environments
04
Nuclear Materials · Radiation Resistance

Radiation resistance of Refractory High-Entropy Alloys.

I aim to develop data-driven strategies for identifying chemical and local-structural characteristics associated with improved radiation tolerance in refractory high-entropy alloys for demanding nuclear environments.
